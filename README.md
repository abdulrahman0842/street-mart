# Street Mart

**Street Mart** is a powerful Flutter-based product listing app that promotes local vendors and businesses. Users can easily share photos and descriptions of the products they've purchased from local shops. This allows other users to discover unique items and supports the visibility of small businesses.

By integrating APIs for user authentication and authorization, product sharing, and offline capabilities, Street Mart offers a seamless experience for its users while also benefiting local commerce.

---

## Features

- **Product Sharing:**  
  Share pictures of products from local vendors, complete with descriptions and vendor/shop locations. This helps local businesses gain more attention.

- **Vendor Promotion:**  
  Promote local shops by showcasing user-purchased products, boosting their online presence.

- **User Authentication:**  
  Secure authentication and authorization via API, using `SharedPreferences` for safe storage of credentials.

- **Offline Mode:**  
  View previously loaded products even without an internet connection, thanks to Hive-powered local storage.

- **State Management:**  
  Utilizes `Provider` for clean, efficient, and manageable state management throughout the app.
